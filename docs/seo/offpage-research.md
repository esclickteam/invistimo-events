## Allowed tactics (after approval)

1. Claim / complete accurate business profiles (Google, directories) with matching NAP and site URL.
2. Ask satisfied customers for genuine reviews on platforms they already use.
3. Pitch original guides (timing, non-replies, seating) to wedding editors — no paid “guest post” spam networks.
4. Co-marketing with venues/producers: case notes, not fabricated ratings.
5. Publish demo videos on the official channel and embed on existing product pages.

## Forbidden

- Buying links or PBNs.
- Fake reviews or incentive-for-review schemes that violate platform rules.
- Publishing as Invistimo on third-party sites without owner approval.
- Adding `sameAs` / AggregateRating / Review schema without verified profiles or real review data.
