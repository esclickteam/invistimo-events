## Strategic takeaways (no fake claims)

1. **SERP is crowded on “free” and fixed low prices.** Invistimo’s honest calculator + package table is the counter-narrative; keep it visible above the fold on `/pricing` and cost guide.
2. **Topic pages win:** rivals have dedicated URLs for WhatsApp RSVP, seating, price compare. Invistimo now mirrors that URL architecture — keep one primary per intent.
3. **AI seating stories are loud.** Only market Invistimo seating features that exist (sketch, manual/group assign, table message). Do not claim sketch-AI unless product ships it.
4. **Directories influence trust** (mit4mit educational pieces already quote Diginet). Listing + real reviews matter as much as on-page SEO.

Monthly: re-run the six AI monitor questions + three Google queries (wedding RSVP, WhatsApp RSVP, seating). Log URL1–3 only — no fabricated ranks for Invistimo until GSC shows queries.
